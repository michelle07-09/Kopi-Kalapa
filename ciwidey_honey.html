The Art of the Honey Process

At Kopi Kalapa, our collaboration with Ciwidey smallholders is a masterclass in patient craftsmanship. By carefully preserving the fruit's mucilage through the drying phase, we unlock the signature 'Red Honey' profile—a delicate equilibrium where the crisp clarity of a washed coffee meets the opulent, jam-like body of a natural. It is a process that demands constant attention, but the syrupy sweetness in your cup makes every hour worth it.

The Hands Behind the Harvest

Heritage in Every Bean

In the high-altitude forests of Ciwidey, West Java, we work closely with a small cooperative of multi-generational farmers. Their dedication to the Red Honey process—carefully leaving a portion of the coffee cherry’s mucilage on during drying—is what gives this roast its signature syrupy sweetness and complex red fruit profile. Every harvest is a testament to the patient craftsmanship of our Bandung farming partners.

How to Brew the Perfect Cup

We recommend these three methods to bring out the bright acidity and floral notes of the Ciwidey Honey Process beans.

The V60 Pour Over

Highlight the red apple acidity. 1:15 ratio, 92°C water.

  • 1.Bloom. Pour 50g of water and wait 30 seconds.
  • 2.Continuous Pour. Gently pour in circular motions up to 250g.
  • 3.Draw Down. Let it finish dripping (approx. 2:30 - 3:00 mins total).
View Guidearrow_forward

The French Press

Maximize the syrupy body. 4-minute steep, coarse grind.

  • 1.Coarse Grind. Add 30g of coarsely ground coffee to the carafe.
  • 2.Bloom & Steep. Pour 500g of hot water. Stir gently and let sit for 4 minutes.
  • 3.Press & Pour. Slowly press the plunger down and serve immediately.
View Guidearrow_forward

The AeroPress

For a balanced, honey-like sweetness. Inverted method.

  • 1.Prepare: Add 15g of medium-fine coffee and 200g of hot water.
  • 2.Steep: Place the plunger and wait for 2 minutes.
  • 3.Press: Press down slowly with consistent pressure for 30 seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is the roast? expand_more

We roast in small batches daily in our Bandung studio. Every bag is shipped within 48 hours of roasting to ensure you receive it at its peak flavor profile.

What is the Honey Process? expand_more

Despite the name, no actual honey is used! It refers to the sticky "mucilage" left on the bean while drying. This process bridges the gap between washed and natural coffees, creating intense sweetness.

Do you ship outside of Indonesia? expand_more

Currently, we focus on domestic shipping within Indonesia to guarantee freshness. We are exploring international shipping options for our wholesale partners.
