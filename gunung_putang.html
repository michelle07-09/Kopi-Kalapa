How to Brew the Perfect Cup

We recommend these three methods to bring out the bright acidity and floral notes of the Gunung Puntang beans.

The V60 Pour Over

Brings out the delicate floral and citrus notes. Clean and bright.

  • 1.Bloom. Pour 50g of water and wait 30 seconds.
  • 2.Continuous Pour. Gently pour in circular motions up to 250g.
  • 3.Draw Down. Let it finish dripping (approx. 2:30 - 3:00 mins total).

The French Press

Highlights the body and sweetness. Full and rich.

  • 1.Coarse Grind. Add 30g of coarsely ground coffee to the carafe.
  • 2.Bloom & Steep. Pour 500g of hot water. Stir gently and let sit for 4 minutes.
  • 3.Press & Pour. Slowly press the plunger down and serve immediately to avoid over-extraction.
View Guidearrow_forward

The AeroPress

Balanced and versatile. Perfect for a quick, concentrated cup.

  • 1.Prepare: Add 15g of medium-fine coffee and 200g of hot water. Stir gently.
  • 2.Steep: Place the plunger and wait for 2 minutes to allow full extraction.
  • 3.Press: Press down slowly with consistent pressure for 30 seconds.
