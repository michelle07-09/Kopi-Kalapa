Rooted in Tradition, Roasted in Bandung.

We believe that great coffee is a collaboration between nature and craftsmanship. Our beans are sourced directly from smallholder farmers across the volcanic slopes of West Java.

By roasting in small batches at our Bandung facility, we ensure that the unique terroir of each origin—from the floral notes of Ciwidey to the deep chocolate of Pangalengan—is meticulously preserved and celebrated in every cup.
