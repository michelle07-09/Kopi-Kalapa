OUR STORY

Artisanal Beans, Rooted in West Java

Cultivating connections from the volcanic soils of Bandung to your morning cup.

EST. 2018

A Heritage of Craft in Bandung

Born in the heart of Bandung, Kopi Kalapa started with a simple belief: coffee should be a deliberate, shared experience. We began as a small-batch roastery driven by an obsession with quality over speed.

Every roast honors the rich history of West Javanese coffee. By prioritizing transparency and community, we strive to elevate the daily ritual of brewing, transforming it into a moment of mindful connection with the land and the hands that nurtured the beans.

Direct Trade Partnerships

We source directly from farmer cooperatives, ensuring fair pay and sustainable practices that empower local communities.

GUNUNG PUNTANG

Puntang Makmur Cooperative

Known for their meticulous washed process, yielding exceptionally clean profiles with bright acidity and floral notes. We've partnered with them since day one.

CIWIDEY

Sunda Hejo Society

Masters of the natural process. Their high-altitude farms produce beans with profound sweetness and complex berry undertones.
